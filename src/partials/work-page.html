Feed Push Monitor

Cameras and AI check that every cow can reach her feed.

The problem

Cows push their feed out of reach as they eat. Workers have to push it back, and if they miss it, cows eat less and give less milk.

What we built

AI watches the feed lanes through cameras. If feed stays out of reach for more than 15 minutes, the farm head gets an alert with a photo as proof.

What the client gets

A live screen showing every shed and how fast workers respond. Owners see what's happening without walking every lane.

Dairy Data Analyst

Ask your herd data a question in plain English.

The problem

The farm's records system is powerful, but staff need special commands to get answers from it.

What we built

Staff type a normal question, such as "Which cows need attention this week?", and get a clear answer.

What the client gets

Quick answers on milk, breeding and health, plus early warnings about trends and risks based on the farm's own history.
