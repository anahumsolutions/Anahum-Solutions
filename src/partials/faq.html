FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does anahum do?

We create AI ad and social content and product visuals, and build automation, AI agents, chatbots and custom AI systems for modern businesses.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We work with brands and businesses that want to save time or create better content, from clothing and fabric brands to farms and sales teams. If you are not sure we are a fit, just ask.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the form below. We reply within 24 hours and agree a plan with you.

What do you need from me?

A short brief, your brand details and clear photos of your product or collection if you want AI visuals.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we give a quote after a short chat about what you need. Message us on WhatsApp and we will get back to you.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the service. AI fashion shoots and product visuals: first samples in 2 to 3 days and the final set 3 to 7 days after you approve. Workflow automation: 1 to 2 weeks for a simple workflow and 2 to 4 weeks for bigger ones. AI agents and chatbots: 2 to 4 weeks. Custom AI systems: usually 4 to 8 weeks or more. We confirm your exact timeline before we start.

Can I see examples first?

Yes. Our Work page shows real projects. If you want to know how we would handle your brand, message us and we will talk it through.

Do you make AI fashion shoots for clothing brands?

Yes. We create realistic photos and reels of models wearing your outfits, without a studio, travel or booking costs. You can see examples on our Work page.

Will a person check what the AI does?

Yes. For anything important, such as sending a message to a customer, a person on your team approves it first.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Revisions are agreed with you before we start and depend on your package.

How does payment work?

We agree the package and payment terms in writing before any work begins.
