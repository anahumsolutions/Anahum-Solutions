Our Working Process

From first message to finished work in four simple steps

01 · Discovery

We listen first

We talk through your business, your goals and what you want to fix or create. You tell us what you have, and we tell you what is possible.

Typical time Day 1–2

We agree the plan

We agree what we will make, how long it will take and what it will cost, in writing. For visuals we send first samples, and for systems we show you a simple plan.

02 · Plan
Typical time 2–3 days
03 · Build

We make it

We create your shoots or build your automation, AI helper or custom system. You see progress along the way and tell us what to change.

Typical time Days to weeks

We launch and support

We hand everything over, show your team how to use it, and stay available to fix and improve it.

04 · Launch
Typical time Ongoing

Typical timings: AI fashion shoots and product visuals, first samples in 2 to 3 days and the final set 3 to 7 days after you approve. Workflow automation, 1 to 2 weeks for a simple workflow and 2 to 4 weeks for bigger ones. AI agents and chatbots, 2 to 4 weeks. Custom AI systems are planned for each project and usually take 4 to 8 weeks or more. We confirm your exact timeline before we start.
