01 · Brand story

Ana + hum

ANA: the core

The central intelligence at the heart of a business. In the icon, it is the solid orange dot.

HUM: the quiet

A constant presence working in the background, like the low hum of a system. In the icon, it is the two rings around the core.

Say it like this: "The solid core is the intelligence of your business. The rings are the hum: quiet, constant, always working."

02 · Logo

Primary lockup

The lowercase "anahum", with the icon as the first letter. Always use the original vector files. Never redraw, retype or rebuild the logo.

Colorways

Dark and light use cream or maroon letters with the orange dot. Black and white are one-color versions, dot included, for print and tight budgets.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the dot's diameter on all sides. Minimum width: 120 px for the lockup. Below that, use the icon alone (minimum 24 px).

Do

Use the dark version on maroon, black and photos. Use the light version on cream and white. Use the white version on orange.

Don't

Stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, add shadows or effects, change the letter case, or place on busy backgrounds.

03 · Color

Palette

Maroon and cream carry about 85% of any design, Ember Orange about 10%, Peach Cream is an occasional soft accent. Maximum 3 colors per post. Gradients: maroon to black, or orange to peach.
